## How we work

**The estate sets the pace.** The court has its own timeline and so does the family. We fit the sale around both instead of pressing for a listing date.

**Everything is written down.** An estate trustee may be asked about a decision years later. Written opinions of value, written recommendations and a log of every offer make that easy to answer.

**We are patient with the contents.** Estate sales often stall because nobody wants to decide what happens to a parent's belongings. We will not rush that and we can bring in people who do this work with care.

## Where this comes up in Toronto

In established areas of detached houses where owners have often stayed for decades, such as Leaside, Lawrence Park, Don Mills and High Park. Our guide to [selling an estate home in Toronto](/selling-an-estate-home-toronto/) covers probate, estate administration tax and the Vacant Home Tax.

## Get the right advice

Estate administration raises legal and tax questions that a real estate brokerage cannot answer. Engage an estates lawyer and an accountant. We will work with them and look after the property.
