## How we work

**The lender comes first.** A plan to move up built without knowing what you can carry is guesswork. One early conversation with your lender avoids the worst outcomes of this kind of move.

**We are straight about conditions.** A condition on the sale of your home protects you. In a competitive offer it will often cost you the house. You should know which case you are in before you write the offer.

**One team runs both sides.** When the sale and the purchase are handled separately, the closing dates are where it goes wrong. We plan both dates together from the first meeting.

## A common Toronto path

From a condo apartment or townhouse into a semi-detached or detached house, often in an established area with room for a growing household.
