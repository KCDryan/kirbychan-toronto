## How we approach the upper market

The costliest error at the top of Toronto is treating it like the middle of the market with larger numbers attached. The pool of buyers, the evidence and the tax all behave differently.

**The value has to hold up.** When several agents quote a price and one is well above the others, that figure is often about winning the listing. We would rather lose the appointment than list at a number the sales do not support.

**Buyers here compare carefully.** People shopping at this level follow a short list of homes for months. They know what sold, what did not and what came back at a lower price.

**Reporting is written and regular.** You get showings, feedback, competing activity and our recommendation in writing, on a set schedule.

## The tax change buyers ask about

From April 1, 2026 the City of Toronto charges municipal land transfer tax of 4.40 percent on the portion of a price between $3 million and $4 million, rising in steps to 8.60 percent above $20 million, on property with one or two single family residences. That is on top of Ontario's land transfer tax. Our [luxury homes guide](/luxury-homes-toronto/) works through examples. The [land transfer tax calculator](/land-transfer-tax-calculator-toronto/) runs any price.
