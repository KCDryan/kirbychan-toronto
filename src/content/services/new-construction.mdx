## How we approach a builder's agreement

**We treat the documents as the product.** A model suite shows finishes. The disclosure statement and agreement show what you are committing to. We keep track of both and of the dates that start your rights.

**We add up the whole cost.** Deposits, interim occupancy fees, closing adjustments and two land transfer taxes on registration are separate amounts that arrive at separate times. We list each one in writing for you and your lawyer.

**We compare against resale.** In August 2026 the TRREB median for a resale condo apartment in the City of Toronto was $550,000. A finished unit with a readable status certificate and a closing date you choose is sometimes the better fit and we will say so.
