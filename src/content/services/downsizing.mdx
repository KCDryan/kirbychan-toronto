## How we work

**Your timetable sets the listing date.** A move that is rushed tends to cost more, in money and in stress. If you need five months to be ready, the plan allows five months.

**We keep preparation spending short.** Most of what owners think a house needs before sale will not come back in the price. We give you a short list and permission to skip the rest.

**We treat the move as the personal decision it is.** Leaving the house where you raised a family is not a routine transaction. We would rather you felt sure than fast.

## Where this comes up in Toronto

Across the city, in areas of detached houses where many owners have lived for decades. Common next steps are condo apartments near the subway in places such as Bayview Village and Willowdale, townhouses or bungalows closer to the old street or a move nearer to children and grandchildren.
