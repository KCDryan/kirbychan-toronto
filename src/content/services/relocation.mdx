## How we work with people moving here

**Budget reality first.** The most common relocation setback is weeks spent viewing in an area that was never affordable for the home you need. We raise that in the first conversation.

**Transit is tested, not assumed.** A route that looks short on the map can be slow at eight in the morning. We point you to the stations and lines that matter for each address and ask you to try the trip at the hour you will make it.

**Trade-offs are stated out loud.** Every Toronto neighbourhood gives and takes something. Our [neighbourhood guides](/neighbourhoods/) describe both sides. Our [relocation guide](/relocating-to-toronto/) covers OHIP, licences and taxes in order.

## Where our relocating buyers come from

Other parts of Ontario, other provinces and other countries. Each group arrives with different assumptions. Buyers from abroad usually need more time on things residents take for granted, such as how conditional offers work here, credit history, school registration and the occupancy declaration Toronto requires from every homeowner each year.
