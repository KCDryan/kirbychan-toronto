## How we work with investors

**We start with the law.** A unit's first occupancy date, its lease and the City licences that may apply shape what it can earn more than the asking price does.

**We show the arithmetic.** You see every cost line and where each figure came from. Where our assumptions differ from yours, you will know which line and why.

**We do not forecast.** We will not tell you what rents or prices will do. We work with what a property costs and what comparable units lease for today.
