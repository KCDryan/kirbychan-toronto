## How we work with first time buyers

**We price both taxes from the first meeting.** Buyers arriving from elsewhere in Ontario often expect one land transfer tax. In the City of Toronto there are two and the rebates are capped, so we show the net figure on each home you consider.

**We separate the rules that look alike.** Ontario's refund and the City's rebate look at your whole life of home ownership. The FHSA looks back over four calendar years. Knowing which applies saves people from claiming money they are not entitled to or missing money they are.

**We tell you what your budget reaches.** If a semi-detached house is beyond your number this year, we would rather say so on the first visit than after six weekends of viewings.
