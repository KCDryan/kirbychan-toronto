faq:
  - q: Do both spouses have to agree before a Toronto home is sold?
    a: For a matrimonial home, Ontario's Family Law Act says a married spouse cannot sell without the other spouse joining in or consenting, unless a separation agreement has released those rights, a court has authorized it or a registered designation applies. This can hold even when only one spouse is on title. A family lawyer can confirm how it applies to you.
  - q: Are the rules the same for common law couples?
    a: No. The Act's definition of spouse for these rules covers married couples. Ontario's guidance says common law couples are not legally required to split property acquired while living together. Ask a family lawyer about your situation.

## How we run these sales

**The same information, at the same time.** Updates, showing feedback and offers go to both owners together. Neither owner has to wonder what the other was told.

**Focused on the sale.** Conversations stay on the decisions the sale requires. If it is easier, we communicate with each owner separately.

**Alongside your lawyers.** The legal questions belong to your lawyers. We keep them up to date on the sale so closing is not held up.

## On timing

Some owners need the sale finished quickly. Others need time before selling makes sense. We explain what the Toronto market is doing and what that means for your timing, then the decision stays with you and your advisers.
