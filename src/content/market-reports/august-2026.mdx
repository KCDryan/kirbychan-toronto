## August 2026 in the City of Toronto

TRREB recorded 1,767 sales in the City of Toronto in August 2026. Across all property types the median price was $770,000 and the average $979,684.

Condo apartments were the largest group, with 885 sales and a median of $550,000. Detached houses followed with 550 sales and a median of $1,170,000.

Sellers brought 4,200 new listings to market during the month and 8,727 listings were active. Homes sold for 97% of their list price on average and had been listed for an average of 35 days.

## Toronto West, Central and East

TRREB splits the city into three districts. They sell very different mixes of homes, which shows in the medians.

| District | Sales | Average price | Median price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Toronto West | 460 | $955,789 | $820,000 |
| Toronto Central | 882 | $1,051,186 | $690,000 |
| Toronto East | 425 | $857,162 | $825,000 |

Source: TRREB Market Watch, August 2026.

Toronto Central had the highest average of the three but the lowest median. Most of its sales are condo apartments, 598 of them in August, while its detached houses carried the highest detached median of the three districts at $1,800,000.
