## The short answer

Agincourt is a mixed market in north Scarborough. TRREB reports it as two communities in its Toronto East district, Agincourt North and Agincourt South-Malvern West. Together they recorded 107 sales from April to June 2026.

The two halves sell different homes. Agincourt North is led by houses, with a median of $847,500 across all property types. Agincourt South-Malvern West is led by condo apartments, with a median of $528,000. Condo apartments were 52 of the 107 sales across both communities and detached houses were 34.

Transit runs on rail and buses rather than the subway. Agincourt GO, on the Stouffville line, sits on Sheppard Avenue East. TTC buses run west along Sheppard to Don Mills station on Line 4 and south to Kennedy station on Line 2.

## What homes cost

The table above lists each community and property type separately. Read the property type rows rather than the two headline medians, because each headline mixes detached houses near $1,000,000 with condo apartments under $500,000.

In Agincourt North, TRREB recorded 45 sales with a median of $847,500 and an average of $794,738. The 17 detached sales had a median of about $1,085,000 and an average of about $1,074,000. Semi-detached houses had 4 sales at a median of about $888,000.

Condo townhouses in Agincourt North had 6 sales at a median of about $684,000. Condo apartments had 13 sales at a median of about $415,000. TRREB also recorded 3 sales in its "other" category at a median of about $978,000 and 2 freehold townhouse sales, too few for a reported price.

In Agincourt South-Malvern West, TRREB recorded 62 sales with a median of $528,000 and an average of $654,948. Condo apartments set the volume, with 39 sales at a median of about $445,000. The 17 detached sales had a median of about $990,000 and an average of about $1,041,000. There were 2 semi-detached sales, 2 condo townhouse sales and 2 other sales, too few for TRREB to report a price. There were no freehold townhouse sales.

The average sits above the median in Agincourt South-Malvern West because detached sales near $1,000,000 pull the average up against a larger group of condo apartments. In Agincourt North the average sits below the median, because the condo apartment and condo townhouse sales pull it down.

The by type figures are read from TRREB's charts, which print values in thousands, so treat them as approximate. With 4 to 17 sales in most rows, a single quarter can move. Compare several quarters before you set a price. Use recent sales in the same building or on the same street where you can.

## Housing stock

The City of Toronto's Neighbourhood Profiles use the same two names as TRREB: Agincourt North and Agincourt South-Malvern West. The 2021 Census shows how different they are.

Agincourt North had 27,540 residents in 9,090 private dwellings. 36.5% of dwellings (3,315) were single-detached houses and 16.5% (1,500) were row houses. 8.7% (795) were semi-detached houses. Only 24.1% (2,195) were in apartment buildings of five or more storeys. 78.5% of households owned their home and 21.5% rented.

Agincourt South-Malvern West had 24,170 residents in 8,895 private dwellings. 44.2% of dwellings (3,935) were in apartment buildings of five or more storeys. 31.0% (2,760) were single-detached houses. 69.6% of households owned their home and 30.4% rented.

The rest of the dwellings in both areas were other Census structure types, which include apartments in duplexes and apartments in buildings under five storeys. The City's heritage conservation district list has no district in Agincourt, so there is no area-wide heritage plan here. Individual properties can still be designated, so check the exact address.

## Transit and getting downtown

Agincourt GO is at 4100 Sheppard Avenue East. GO Transit lists it on the Stouffville line, with trains toward Union Station by way of Kennedy GO and trains north toward Unionville GO. The station has free parking with 337 spaces in the main lot, carpool parking, bike racks and secured bike rooms. GO Transit also lists TTC connections at the station.

The 85 Sheppard East bus runs along Sheppard Avenue East from Don Mills station on Line 4. It stops at Kennedy Road and at Reidmount Avenue beside Agincourt GO, then continues east past Midland Avenue, Brimley Road and McCowan Road. The 985 Sheppard East Express runs the same corridor from Don Mills station with fewer stops, including the Agincourt GO stop.

North and south, the 43 Kennedy and 57 Midland buses both run to Kennedy station. The 129 McCowan North bus runs to Kennedy station by way of Scarborough Centre station. The TTC groups the 129 McCowan North and the 903 Kennedy Stn-Scarborough Express with its Line 3 replacement service.

To reach Union station on the TTC, ride Line 2 west from Kennedy station to Bloor-Yonge station, which is 13 stops. Change to Line 1 and ride south to Union station, another 6 stops. That is 19 stops on the TTC map, plus the bus ride to Kennedy station.

GO and TTC service levels change by time of day. Check the GO schedule and the TTC trip from the exact address at the hour you would travel before you rely on either.

## Parks, libraries and everyday life

The Agincourt Community Recreation Centre is at 31 Glen Watford Drive, beside Agincourt Park. The City lists two indoor rinks, indoor pools and a sauna there. It is also home to the Agincourt Lawn Bowling Club and the Agincourt Tennis Club. The City notes that the centre is being improved, so check the facility page for current programs.

Agincourt Park at 27 Glen Watford Drive has a playground, tennis courts and washrooms. In Agincourt North the City lists Iroquois Park at 295 Chartland Boulevard South, Alexmuir Park at 205 Alexmuir Boulevard, Richmond Park at 75 Richmond Park Boulevard and Brimley Woods at 2755 Brimley Road. In Agincourt South-Malvern West it lists Farquharson Park at 250 Pitfield Road and McDairmid Woods Park at 51 Rubic Crescent. Farquharson Park has a cricket pitch.

The Commander Recreation Centre at 140 Commander Boulevard has two indoor rinks. The City notes it sits in an industrial area with transit only during rush hour.

The Toronto Public Library's Agincourt branch is at 155 Bonis Avenue and its Woodside Square branch is at 1571 Sandhurst Circle. TPL says plans are underway to relocate and expand the Woodside Square branch.

## Downsizing in Agincourt

Agincourt has a large stock of smaller homes. From April to June 2026 TRREB recorded 52 condo apartment sales across the two communities, plus 6 condo townhouse sales and 4 semi-detached sales in Agincourt North.

An owner selling a detached house in Agincourt North at about the detached median of $1,085,000 and buying a condo apartment in the same community at about $415,000 would, on these figures, free up a large share of the sale price. The same step in Agincourt South-Malvern West runs from about $990,000 to about $445,000.

Condo townhouses in Agincourt North, at a median of about $684,000 across 6 sales, are the step down for owners who want ground-level entry and less upkeep. The 2021 Census counted 1,500 row houses in Agincourt North, which is part of that stock.

Buyers who want to compare a subway location can look at [Don Mills](/don-mills-toronto/), where the 85 Sheppard East bus begins at Don Mills station. [Bayview Village](/bayview-village-toronto/) sits further west on Line 4. Our [downsizing guide](/downsizing-toronto/) covers the order of selling and buying and the costs of both.

## What Agincourt offers

Agincourt offers two housing markets in one area. Agincourt North sells mainly detached houses, condo townhouses and condo apartments, with a detached median of about $1,085,000 from April to June 2026. Agincourt South-Malvern West sells mainly condo apartments at a median of about $445,000, with detached houses at about $990,000.

The housing stock matches the sales. Agincourt North was 36.5% single-detached in the 2021 Census and Agincourt South-Malvern West was 44.2% apartments in buildings of five or more storeys.

For transit, Agincourt GO on the Stouffville line runs trains to Union Station. TTC buses run along Sheppard Avenue East to Don Mills station on Line 4 and south to Kennedy station on Line 2, 19 stops from Union station. Local facilities include the Agincourt Community Recreation Centre, City parks in both communities and the Agincourt and Woodside Square library branches.
